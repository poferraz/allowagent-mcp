# Inspiration and reset worksheet

The resources page offers five official directions. They are prompts, not requirements.

## 1. AI agents or automation

Use PayPal to take an approved action on a user's behalf. The page points to shopping and checkout agents, automatic contractor payment after a job is complete, and agent-to-agent settlement.

Good question: **What transaction can become safe and obvious once an agent handles the repetitive coordination?**

## 2. Money movement experiences

Make sending, receiving, splitting, or tracking money faster, simpler, or more flexible. The examples include phone-number transfers, freelancer payment dashboards, instant gig payouts, receipt-based splitting, and one donation split across causes.

Good question: **Where does a person lose time or confidence between “someone owes money” and “the right person received it”?**

## 3. Business solutions

Help merchants, freelancers, creators, platforms, or marketplace operators run the business. The examples include multi-currency invoices, tipping or memberships, marketplace splits, and point-of-sale add-ons.

Good question: **Which small business decision is currently hidden inside payment records, email, and spreadsheets?**

## 4. Consumer experiences

Make it easier for people to pay, get paid, or understand their money. The examples include automatic transaction categorization, requesting money inside chat, and checkout for an underserved niche.

Good question: **What payment moment feels unnecessarily technical, repetitive, or stressful for a specific group?**

## 5. Social impact ideas

Use PayPal to expand access or drive good. The examples include accessible multilingual donations, microloans and financial inclusion, recurring donor payouts to field partners, and neighborhood pooling for shared projects.

Good question: **Which good outcome is blocked by payment coordination rather than donor intent?**

## Fast screen

Score each idea from 1 to 5:

| Test | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Payment leverage | Does PayPal do something central in the demo? |
| AI necessity | Would a rule-based form or dashboard miss the key value? |
| User pain | Can one user describe the problem in one sentence? |
| Demo clarity | Can the before/after be shown in three minutes? |
| Feasibility | Can one person build the slice at 5–8 hours per week? |
| Trust | Can every consequential action be reviewed and verified? |

Keep the top two or three cards. Discard ideas that need a marketplace, a whole industry workflow, or a large dataset before the first useful transaction.
