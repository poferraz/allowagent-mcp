## Shortlist for a first prototype

- Pick **AG Grid** if the transaction story becomes clearer when users can see a live dashboard.
- Pick **Render** if the product needs webhooks or delayed processing.
- Pick **Zapier** if the differentiator is a useful action across existing business tools.
- Pick **Elastic** if the differentiator is finding the right evidence in messy records.
- Pick **APIMatic** and **Postman** as build aids even when they are not part of the final user-facing demo.
- Leave **KERNEL**, **Astropods**, and **Bryntum** for ideas whose core value actually requires their specialized runtime, agent, or scheduling surface.
